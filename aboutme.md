Hi, I'm a final-year student at the **Academy of Cryptography Techniques in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam**, with a growing interest in cybersecurity, particularly **Offensive Security**.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey in this field, constantly learning, experimenting, and improving my technical skills. I created this blog as a personal space to document my learning process, share useful knowledge, and reflect on the things I build, practice, and research.

### What I'm Interested In

My current interests include:

- **Offensive Security:** Learning about penetration testing methodologies, attack techniques, and security assessment.
- **Hands-on Labs:** Practicing technical skills through labs, experiments, and security-focused challenges.
- **Security Research:** Exploring vulnerabilities, analyzing security concepts, and understanding how systems can be exploited and secured.
- **Tools & Techniques:** Learning how security tools work and documenting practical use cases, findings, and lessons learned.

### What You'll Find Here

This blog is where I share notes, write-ups, lab experiences, technical experiments, and research findings from my learning journey.

Some posts may be practical walkthroughs, while others may be explanations of concepts, lessons learned from troubleshooting, or personal research notes. My goal is to turn what I learn into useful, well-documented resources that may help others who are on a similar path.

I believe that understanding security requires more than just reading theory. It takes hands-on practice, curiosity, critical thinking, and a willingness to keep learning.

### My Philosophy

I'm not here to claim that I know everything. Instead, I want to document my progress, share what I discover, and learn from the cybersecurity community along the way.

Everything published here reflects my understanding at the time of writing. I aim to keep my work technical, transparent, and focused on responsible security learning and research.

If you're also exploring cybersecurity or Offensive Security, I hope you find something useful here.

**Learn continuously. Practice deliberately. Share knowledge.**
